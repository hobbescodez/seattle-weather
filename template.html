How this works (and why the honesty)

Most weather apps hand you one confident number. This one shows an estimate and how sure we actually are — because a few hours out, nobody knows the temperature to the degree.

It's a real model: it reads live observations from {{obs_station_label}}, measures the recent temperature trend, adjusts for where we are in the day's natural rise and fall, blends in the National Weather Service's own hourly forecast for {{location_name}}, and watches stations around Puget Sound for an incoming marine push or heat surge. When those signals get noisy, the range widens — and we tell you why instead of hiding it.
