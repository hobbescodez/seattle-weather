How does this work? (and why the honesty)

Most weather apps hand you a single confident number. This one shows you an estimate and how sure we actually are — because a few hours out, nobody really knows the temperature to the degree.

Under the hood it's a real model: it reads live observations from Boeing Field (KBFI), ~5 mi south, measures the recent temperature trend, and adjusts for where we are in the day's natural rise and fall (temperatures behave very differently at dawn versus mid-afternoon). It then blends in the National Weather Service's own hourly forecast for Fremont's exact location, and watches weather stations around Puget Sound for early signs of a marine push or a heat surge moving in.

When those signals get noisy, the estimate's range widens — and we tell you why, instead of hiding it behind a clean-looking number.
